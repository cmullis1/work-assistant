Urgent
- Definition: An event that critically impacts business operations, causing system/service unavailability and a complete loss of trading ability or revenue impact. The site is not operational.
- Examples:
  - None of the location's devices can run the product.
  - Site is unable to accept their primary form of payment, such as the site only accepts credit card payments but cannot process them.
  - Site is unable to complete any sales.
  - Server crashed or is unavailable.
  - Database was corrupted, or database is full.

High
- Definition: An event with significant business, trading or revenue impact where the system/service is operational but limited, and key functions are unavailable.
- Examples:
  - More than 10% of the location's devices cannot run the product.
  - 50% or more of the location's checkout stations are not operational.
  - Prices or discounts are applied incorrectly on sales.
  - Recurring issues which cause significant operational impact, and there is a known temporary solution or work-around.

Normal
- Definition: An event with moderate business, trading or revenue impact; the system/service is available and operational, but some non-essential functions are unavailable or not working as expected.
- Examples:
  - Fewer than 10% of the location's devices cannot run the product.
  - A single user is unable to log in.
  - Fewer than 50% of the location's checkout stations are not operational.
  - The sales screen freezes when finalizing a transaction.
  - Printer is not printing receipts.
  - Scanners are not functioning.
  - Customer accounts not syncing across locations consistently.

Low
- Definition: An event with no impact on trading ability or revenue.
- Examples:
  - Cosmetic changes, such as on-screen images.
  - Reports issues:
    - Webpage is not loading.
    - Discrepancies on reports.
    - Automated reports did not go out.
    - Modification to automated reports.
  - Requests for training on the product.
  - Correcting issues with backups.
  - Site health checks.
